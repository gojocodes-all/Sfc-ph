Privacy Policy

How PICNYM handles information.

Effective: 8 August 2026 · Last updated: 8 August 2026

PICNYM is designed so inbox owners do not see the identity of anonymous senders. That is different from promising that no technical information is ever processed.

1. Information you provide

If you create an account, PICNYM may process your email address, account identifier, display name and authentication information handled by the authentication provider. If you use Google sign-in, Google may provide basic account information such as your name, email address and profile information according to the permissions you approve.

Content submitted through PICNYM can include text messages, image files, audio or voice-note files, poll questions, poll options, votes and inbox-owner replies.

2. Technical and usage information

PICNYM may process limited technical information such as IP-derived network information, browser or user-agent information, timestamps, generated device identifiers and hashed abuse-prevention identifiers. This information may be used for rate limiting, spam control, duplicate-vote prevention, blocking abusive senders, security monitoring and service reliability.

Where possible, PICNYM uses one-way hashes or generated identifiers instead of exposing raw technical identifiers in normal product features.

3. What inbox owners can see

An inbox owner can see the content you send, the approximate time it was received and whether a sender has been blocked by that owner. PICNYM does not provide ordinary inbox owners with your email address, account profile, IP address or raw device information merely because you sent an anonymous message.

4. Accounts and local device storage

PICNYM uses browser storage to keep sessions, installation state, voting state and legacy inbox-owner access tokens. Account sessions may persist so you do not have to sign in every time. You can sign out to end the active account session on a device.

5. How information is used

  • Provide and operate anonymous inboxes, polls, media uploads and account access.
  • Authenticate users and keep inbox ownership available across devices.
  • Prevent spam, automated abuse, repeated votes and misuse.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure and improve the service.
  • Respond to valid legal, safety or support requests where required.

6. Service providers

PICNYM uses third-party infrastructure providers to operate the service. This can include hosting, authentication, database, storage and content-delivery services such as Supabase and Vercel. Those providers may process data as necessary to deliver their infrastructure services and under their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

7. Public and shared content

Anonymous inbox content is intended for the inbox owner, but the owner can choose to share message or reply cards publicly. Poll links can also be shared with others. Do not submit information that you would be seriously harmed by if the recipient decided to share it.

8. Retention and deletion

Content may remain stored until it is deleted by the inbox owner, removed for safety or operational reasons, or deleted under an applicable request. Backups and infrastructure caches may retain data for a limited additional period. Account deletion and broader data-export or deletion controls may be expanded as PICNYM develops.

9. Children and teenagers

PICNYM is intended for users aged 13 and above. Where a country requires parental or guardian consent for a minor to use an online service, the user must have that consent. PICNYM should not be used to collect sensitive personal information from children.

10. Security

PICNYM uses access controls, authentication, HTTPS, request limits and other technical safeguards. No internet service can guarantee perfect security, and users should avoid submitting passwords, financial credentials or highly sensitive personal information through anonymous messages.

11. Your choices

You can choose not to create an account, although account-only features may then be unavailable. Inbox owners can delete messages and block senders. Account users can sign out and update basic profile information. Privacy requests can be sent to the project developer.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as PICNYM adds features, providers or legal requirements. The effective or last-updated date will be changed when the policy is materially revised.
