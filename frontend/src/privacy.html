Privacy Policy

How PICNYM handles information.

Effective: 8 August 2026 · Last updated: 8 August 2026

PICNYM is designed so inbox owners do not receive hidden sender identities simply because someone sends an anonymous message. PICNYM still processes limited technical information needed to run, secure and moderate the service.

1. Account and profile information

If you create an account, PICNYM may process your email address, authentication identifier, display name, username, bio, profile picture, theme and privacy preferences, friend relationships and account plan status. Authentication may be handled by providers such as Supabase and Google when you choose Google sign-in.

2. Content you provide

Content submitted through PICNYM can include text, images, audio or voice notes, polls, votes, inbox-owner replies, public answered posts, reports and inbox settings such as hidden words. Content is processed so the requested feature can work.

3. Anonymous messages and voluntary profile reveal

By default, an anonymous message does not show the sender's PICNYM account profile to the inbox owner. A signed-in sender may deliberately choose to reveal their PICNYM profile with a particular message. That choice is optional and applies only when the sender actively selects it.

PICNYM Premium does not provide inbox owners with a hidden sender's email address, exact location, IP address or device fingerprint. Aggregate Premium analytics may count messages, trends and pseudonymous unique senders without converting those signals into a real-world identity.

4. Technical and abuse-prevention information

PICNYM and its infrastructure may process IP/network information, browser or user-agent information, timestamps, generated client identifiers and one-way hashed anti-abuse identifiers. These signals can be used for rate limiting, spam control, duplicate-vote prevention, blocking, security monitoring and aggregate analytics.

Normal product interfaces are designed to avoid exposing raw technical identifiers to other users.

5. Friends and discoverability

If profile discoverability is enabled, signed-in users may find your PICNYM username and public profile information. Friend requests and accepted friendships are stored so friend-only inboxes and social features can work. You can disable discoverability or new friend requests in Settings.

6. What inbox owners can see and control

Inbox owners can see message content, timestamps, their own replies and moderation state such as favorites, archive, reports and blocks. They can pause an inbox, restrict it to accounts or friends, disable selected media types, filter hidden words, delete messages and permanently delete an inbox.

7. Public and shared content

An inbox owner can deliberately publish a replied message to their public PICNYM profile or share generated image cards elsewhere. Poll links can also be shared. Public profile information and voluntarily published answers can be viewed by people who receive or open those links.

8. Browser and device storage

PICNYM uses browser storage for sessions, theme choice, installation state, voting state and legacy inbox-owner tokens. Account sessions may persist until you sign out, clear site data or the session expires.

9. Service providers

PICNYM uses third-party infrastructure for hosting, authentication, databases, storage and delivery, including services such as Supabase and Vercel. Those providers may process data as required to provide their services under their own applicable policies and agreements.

10. Retention, export and deletion

Messages remain until removed by an inbox owner or by PICNYM for safety, legal or operational reasons. Account users can delete individual inboxes and can permanently delete their PICNYM account through Account Settings. Account deletion removes account-owned inboxes and their stored messages and media from the active service, although limited infrastructure backups or logs may persist temporarily under provider retention practices.

PICNYM also provides an account-settings export for basic profile, settings and inbox metadata. Additional export tools may be included with future plans.

11. PICNYM Premium and billing

PICNYM may offer a Premium plan. Until a payment provider is activated, the Billing section is informational and no subscription charge is processed by PICNYM. When payments are added, this policy will be updated to explain the payment processor and billing data involved.

12. Children and teenagers

PICNYM is intended for users aged 13 and above. Where local law requires parental or guardian consent for a minor to use an online service, the user must have that consent. Users should not submit highly sensitive personal information through anonymous messages.

13. Security and safety

PICNYM uses authentication, access controls, HTTPS, request limits, moderation controls and other safeguards. No internet service can promise perfect security. Do not send passwords, financial credentials or information that could seriously harm you if shared.

14. Your choices

You can change profile discoverability, friend requests and theme preferences; pause or restrict inboxes; manage hidden words; block or report messages; delete inboxes; remove your profile picture; sign out; or delete your account. A sender can stay anonymous or voluntarily reveal their PICNYM profile on a message.

15. Changes and contact

This policy may change as PICNYM adds features, providers or legal requirements. Material revisions will update the date above.
