PICNYM features

Anonymous messaging, built like a real social product.

Create an account, build your profile, manage multiple anonymous inboxes, connect with friends and receive text, photos, voice notes and polls with strong safety controls.

Create your accountRead safety rules

Text, photos & voice notes

Receive anonymous text, supported images and voice notes through the same shareable link.

Anonymous polls

Create polls with shareable links and collect votes without showing voter identities to the poll creator.

Profiles & profile pictures

Choose a username, display name, bio and profile picture. Public profile pages can show answers you deliberately publish.

Friends

Find discoverable PICNYM users by username, send requests and create inboxes that accept messages only from accepted friends.

Opt-in identity reveal

A sender can voluntarily attach their PICNYM profile to one message. Leaving the option off keeps the message anonymous to the inbox owner.

Professional inbox controls

Pause a link, require signed-in users or friends, disable selected media types, filter hidden words, block senders, report abuse and delete entire inboxes.

Search, favorites & archive

Search received messages, filter by type, favorite useful messages, archive old ones and keep your dashboard organized.

Shareable image cards

Turn text, media messages and replies into branded image cards, including multi-select sharing.

Light & dark themes

Use a dedicated theme toggle or follow your device theme. Account settings remember your preference.

Portable account ownership

Account-owned inboxes follow your PICNYM account across devices instead of depending on one browser's local storage.

Installable mobile experience

Install PICNYM as a progressive web app on supported phones while using the same account, links and inboxes.

PICNYM Premium

Planned at ₦500/month with larger inbox limits, advanced non-identifying analytics, premium profile styling, exports and creator organization tools. Payments are not active yet.

Privacy promise

Premium does not sell anonymous identities.

PICNYM does not turn hidden sender identity, exact location, IP addresses or device fingerprints into a paid reveal feature. Premium analytics are aggregate and non-identifying. A sender's PICNYM profile is shown only when that sender deliberately chooses to reveal it with a message.
