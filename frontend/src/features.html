PICNYM features

Anonymous messaging, built like a real product.

One account, multiple inboxes and four ways to speak—with practical controls for the person receiving it.

Create your accountRead safety rules

Text, photos & voice

Receive text, supported images and voice notes through the same shareable link.

Anonymous polls

Create polls with shareable links and collect votes without displaying voter identities to the creator.

Prompt deck

Share a ready-made conversation starter or let a sender tap a prompt and edit it before sending.

Private-first replies

Messages stay in your private inbox unless you deliberately reply and publish an answer to your profile.

Profiles & friends

Choose a username, bio and photo, connect with PICNYM users and create friend-only inboxes.

Optional profile reveal

A signed-in sender can attach their profile to one message. Leaving the option off keeps it anonymous to the recipient.

Creator controls

Pause a link, require accounts or friends, disable media types, filter hidden words, block senders and report abuse.

Inbox organization

Search by content, filter by type, favorite useful messages and archive older ones.

Share kit

Turn replies into redesigned branded image cards for sharing without exposing a hidden sender.

Flexible interface

Use light or dark theme, a compact inbox and reduced-motion mode on supported devices.

Cross-device ownership

Account-owned inboxes follow your PICNYM login instead of depending on one browser.

Web and native Android

Use the installable web experience or the separate native Android app with the same account and links.

Privacy promise

No paid identity reveals.

PICNYM Premium will not turn a hidden sender’s email, exact location, IP address or device fingerprint into a paid reveal feature. Any future analytics will remain aggregate and non-identifying.
