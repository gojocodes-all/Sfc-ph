Safety

Anonymous should not mean consequence-free.

Last updated: 8 August 2026

PICNYM hides a sender's profile from the inbox owner, but it does not promise absolute technical anonymity. Limited technical information may be processed to prevent spam, duplicate voting and abuse.

What is not allowed

Do not use PICNYM to harass, threaten, impersonate, stalk, blackmail, exploit, defraud or deliberately endanger another person. Do not upload illegal content, non-consensual intimate content, sexual content involving minors, malware, or content that violates another person's rights.

Inbox controls

Inbox owners can delete messages and block an abusive sender from sending more messages to that inbox. These controls are designed to work without revealing the sender's identity to the recipient.

Anonymous does not mean invisible to infrastructure

PICNYM and its infrastructure providers may process technical data such as network information, browser information, timestamps and generated identifiers for security, rate limiting, duplicate-vote prevention and abuse control. PICNYM does not display this data to ordinary inbox owners.

Age and responsible use

PICNYM is intended for people aged 13 and above. If local law requires parental or guardian permission for a minor to use an online service, that permission is required. Never use PICNYM to pressure someone to disclose private information or participate in unsafe activity.
