About PICNYM

A simpler anonymous inbox, with more ways to speak.

PICNYM started with a basic question: why should anonymous messaging be limited to plain text? The product combines messages, photos, voice notes and polls behind one shareable link.

The idea

Give people a lightweight anonymous inbox without turning the experience into a noisy social network. Sending should be quick. Receiving and sharing should be useful.

The approach

Privacy-aware product design, simple interfaces, abuse controls, account-based ownership and a mobile-friendly web app that works from a link.

Built independently in Lagos, Nigeria.

PICNYM is designed and developed by Owojuyigbe Oluwajomiloju, the developer behind GOJO.DEV. Product development, interface design and engineering are handled as an independent project.
