About PICNYM

Honest messages. More ways to say them.

PICNYM combines text, photos, voice notes and polls behind one link, while giving the inbox owner clear control over what arrives and what becomes public.

The idea

Make sending quick and receiving useful without turning anonymous messaging into a noisy feed.

The approach

Private-first replies, clear abuse controls, account-based ownership and focused interfaces for web and native Android.

Built independently in Lagos, Nigeria.

PICNYM is designed and developed by Owojuyigbe Oluwajomiloju, the developer behind GOJO.DEV.
